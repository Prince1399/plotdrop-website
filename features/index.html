Feature Map

What every PlotDrop feature does, and where to find it in the app. No more hunting through tabs: if it is listed here, this page tells you exactly where it lives.

Everything below is live in the latest build, 1.6.31 (Android sideload now; iPhone build 28, version 1.6.29, live for internal testers, public link after Apple approval).

New in 1.6.31

The welcome banner becomes the neon entrance it was meant to be.

Welcome banner, neon editionLive in 1.6.31

The home banner is now the neon artwork: pink and cyan smoke, a glowing neon border, and the message front and center - Your next favorite drop is waiting. No Explore Now button, nothing dead to tap. The greeting still shows your name.

Where: Home tab, top of the feed.

New in 1.6.30

The welcome banner becomes an entrance, drops stop flashing disappeared, and the whole app gets faster.

Welcome banner redesignLive in 1.6.30

The home banner is now an entrance, not an ad: atmospheric navy with purple and cyan light, no giant logo, and the message front and center - Your next favorite drop is waiting. No button, nothing dead to tap.

Where: Home tab, top of the feed.

Drop detail loading fixLive in 1.6.30

Opening a drop no longer flashes "This drop disappeared" while it is still loading. You see a loading screen until the drop resolves, and the missing message only shows when it is really gone.

Where: Any drop link or shared drop.

Speed round 4Live in 1.6.30

Posts merge by id on home, profiles scroll on a virtualized list, chat attachments load on signed links, and long chats open on the newest 100 messages.

Where: Everywhere - it just feels faster.

Feature guide updatedLive in 1.6.30

Settings now explains every 1.6.28 and 1.6.29 feature: circle search, invite links, private circles, follow requests, scheduled drops, incognito chat, mutual friends, and the rest.

Where: Settings, "Every feature, explained".

Receipt privacy hardeningLive in 1.6.30

Hidden receipt proofs move to private storage with signed links, so a hidden proof stays hidden until the reveal. Activates with the 1.6.30 database update.

Where: Revealed drop cards, receipt photo proof.

New in 1.6.29

Notifications that actually take you somewhere, and circle moderation that cannot happen by accident.

Notifications open the activityLive in 1.6.29

Tapping a notification now opens exactly what it is about: a like or re-drop opens that post, a comment opens that post, a follow opens that person's profile, a DM opens that chat, a party alert opens that party lobby. No more dead taps.

Where: Notifications tab, and push notifications from outside the app.

Circle moderation behind long-pressLive in 1.6.29

Remove and Block no longer sit out on the member row where a stray tap could hit them. Long-press a member's name and they pop up in a menu, and the destructive choice still asks you to confirm first.

Where: Any circle page you own, member list, long-press a member.

New in 1.6.28

Circles grow up, profiles get real privacy, and the whole app moves faster.

Circle searchLive in 1.6.28

Find any circle by name or description from the circles dashboard, no more scrolling as circles grow. Invite-only circles stay hidden from search by design.

Where: Circles tab, search bar at the top.

Circle invites by link and QRLive in 1.6.28

Every circle has a shareable invite link and QR code. Scanning one opens the circle: public circles join right away, private circles send a request, invite-only circles let you in on the link alone.

Where: Any circle page, invite button. Scan from the scanner tab.

True member counts and owner moderationLive in 1.6.28

Member counts now show before you join, not zero until you do. Circle owners can remove members and block members, and a block is enforced by the database: blocked members cannot rejoin or re-request until unblocked.

Where: Circles dashboard counts, and member controls on your circle pages.

Private profile follow requestsLive in 1.6.28

Flip your profile private and a follow becomes a request: you accept or deny it, and only then does the follow exist. Public profiles still follow directly, exactly as before.

Where: Your profile privacy toggle, and the follow requests inbox on your profile.

Scheduled dropsLive in 1.6.28

Schedule any drop for whenever: now, in an hour, in six hours, tomorrow, or a custom time. It stays hidden until its time, then publishes on its own. Posts are excluded, posts always post immediately.

Where: New Drop tab, schedule picker. Your scheduled drops live on your own profile until they publish.

Incognito chatLive in 1.6.28

Flip a chat incognito and messages sent while it is on show as Incognito with no name and no photo, and they delete themselves after one day. Either side can flip the toggle.

Where: Any DM chat, incognito toggle row.

Mutual friendsLive in 1.6.28

See the people you and someone else both follow, as a count with faces on their profile.

Where: Any other user's profile.

New categoriesLive in 1.6.28

Six more plot categories: Comedy, Random, School, Food, Travel, Animals. Random is the catch-all for plots that fit nowhere else.

Where: New Drop tab, category picker.

Faster home feedLive in 1.6.28

Tapping home now shows your saved feed instantly and refreshes quietly underneath, instead of blocking you behind a loading screen. Live updates keep working, they just stop getting in your way.

Where: Everywhere, but you will feel it most on the home tab.

Drops: the game

A drop is a short story with the ending held back. Everyone reads the same story, calls the twist, and waits for the reveal.

Create a drop

Write the story, set the real twist, add a few decoy plots, pick how long until the reveal, and choose who can see it: everyone (Public) or one private group (Inner Circle). Add a photo or a video if the story needs one.

Where: New Drop tab (the + in the bottom bar).

Predict the twist

Read the story and lock in your call before the timer runs out: which plot is the real one? Misclicked? You get two changes before the reveal, and the vote counts move with your pick. For party drops, the fastest-correct bonus runs from your last change, not your first click.

Where: Tap any drop card in the Drops feed, then pick a plot under "What happened next?"

The reveal

When the countdown hits zero, the real twist drops for everyone at the same time. Right calls earn points, and the card shows how the whole crowd voted.

Where: The same drop card, once its timer ends. Revealed drops stay readable in the feed.

Receipt Flip

Attach one hidden proof photo (or a 15-second video) to a drop. Nobody sees it until the reveal, then it lands on the revealed card. You can see your own receipt before the reveal, marked only-you.

Where: New Drop tab, attach a receipt; the revealed card after the flip.

Re-drop, like, comment, share

Re-drop a story to your own feed so your people see it. Like and comment on drops, and share one outside the app with a link back to PlotDrop. The re-drop count on a card counts every re-drop, including zero.

Where: On any drop card, and on the full drop screen (tap the card).

Points and titles

Right calls earn points. Your title is computed fresh from your record every time it loads: ROOKIE, LONG SHOT, RISING, SHARPSHOOTER, and ORACLE for the most accurate callers. Streaks reward players who keep calling it right.

Where: Your Profile tab, and on anyone's profile header.

Home refresh, whole photos, streak parity

Tapping the Home tab refreshes the feed and jumps back to the top. Drop photos attach whole, never cropped. Streak icons match on other profiles, and the video player has -5s/+5s skip plus a Look panel (Normal, Warm, Cool, Dusk).

Where: Home tab, New Drop tab, profiles, video player.

Spotlight drops

Spotlight drops carry a SPOTLIGHT banner and flow through the feed newest-first with everything else: new posts land above them and they sink naturally. A spotlighted drop is notified to all.

Where: In the Drops feed, marked with the SPOTLIGHT banner.

Report and block

Report a drop that breaks the rules, or block someone you do not want to hear from. Reports are acted on within 24 hours. Blocked users can be unblocked later.

Where: On a drop card, and in Settings under Unblock.

Posts: moments

Posts are plain moments, not stories: no twist, no reveal, no voting. For a story with a hidden ending, drop it instead.

Create a post

Share a moment as text, a photo, or both. Text alone is fine, photo alone is fine. Posts never ask for a prediction.

Where: Home feed, tap "Share a moment..." at the top.

Post cards

Posts carry a POST chip so they never blur with drops. Like, comment, and share them from the card. Long-press your own post to delete it.

Where: In the Drops feed, mixed newest-first with drops and re-drops.

Home plus buttonLive in 1.6.27

A plus button in the Share bar opens a menu: Take photo, Record video, Photo library, Video library. Pick one and the post screen opens with that picker already open. Tapping the bar's text still opens the plain composer.

Where: Home feed, the plus in the "Share a moment..." bar.

Video

Video is a drop (or a post), not a separate reels section. Originals stay original: high definition, no downscale, no crop, vertical stays vertical.

Video drops

Attach a video up to 90 seconds to a drop. Over-long videos are rejected, never silently cut. In the feed, the video shows a poster frame with a play button and a length badge: tap to play, never autoplay. While playing, swipe sideways for looks and tap the speed button for 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, or 2x. Playback works for everybody.

Where: New Drop tab, "Attach a video to your drop (up to 90 seconds)".

Video postsLive in 1.6.27

The same video, on a plain post: see something nice and post it from Home without creating a drop. One combined "Add a photo/video" row on the post screen, one media slot per post, and the same tap-to-play frame on the post card.

Where: Home feed, "Share a moment...", then "Add a photo/video - camera or library".

Party reveal soundsLive in 1.6.27

A rising buildup plays in the last seconds before the twist drops, then a hit lands with the truth. Original sounds built for PlotDrop, and they play even on vibrate or silent (mute them any time in Settings).

Where: Inside any party lobby at reveal time.

Drop PartyLive in 1.6.27

The reveal, not the vote, is the party. A Drop Party turns one drop's reveal into a live event inside a circle.

Party drops

Flip the party toggle when you create a drop, in any circle. The party card sits on Home, right under the composer bar, with a live countdown to the reveal.

Where: New Drop tab (party toggle), then the party card on the Home feed.

The lobby and the ball drop

Before the reveal, the party lobby links to the circle's chat so the crew can talk it up. At zero, the ball drops: that is the twist dropping, live, for everyone in the party. No confetti. Emoji reactions fly at the reveal.

Where: The party card on Home, then the party screen.

Live leaderboard and bonus points

The party screen carries a live leaderboard. On top of normal right-call points, the fastest right call earns +5, second +3, third +1. The winner is crowned on the card, and the party stays up as a badge with a replay afterward.

Where: The party screen, during and after the reveal.

Ball-count and sound test

The last 5 seconds of a party countdown count down big on screen (5-4-3-2-1), and the truth lands directly when the server flips, no second countdown. Replays keep the full 3-2-1. Test the reveal sound any time from Settings.

Where: The party lobby at reveal time; Settings, test sound.

Party Nights

Three to five party drops back to back, one night, one overall champion across the whole night.

Where: Party cards on Home, grouped as one night.

Party reminders

A local reminder 10 minutes before a party starts (set when you view the party) and a push when the twist drops. Spectators are welcome: you can watch a party without playing.

Where: Push notifications, controlled in Settings under Push notifications.

Daily TwistLive in 1.6.27

One shared mystery drop for everybody, every day. Same twist, same reveal, one global leaderboard. The daily ritual, and the way new users learn the whole loop by playing it.

Play the daily twist

A new twist posts at 12:00 PM CT and reveals at 6:00 PM CT the same day. Categories rotate daily (comedy, family, humor, everyday life, food, animals, school and work, light relationships) and the content stays fun only: no politics, no mean-spirited stuff, no real-life tragedy.

Where: The Daily Twist card in the feed, every day at lunch.

Circles

Private groups for your people. Drops inside a circle stay inside the circle.

Create and join circles

Create a private circle, invite your people, and drop stories only they can see. Circle drops never hit the global feed.

Where: Circles tab, "Create a Circle".

Circle chat

Every circle has its own group chat. Member photos show in the chat, and party lobbies link right to it.

Where: Open a circle from the Circles tab, then Chat.

Inner Circle audience

When you create a drop, choose Public ("Everyone can predict") or Inner Circle ("Private group only"). Inner Circle drops stay out of the global feed entirely.

Where: New Drop tab, "Who can see it?"

Messages

Direct messages and group chats, with an unread badge that keeps count for you.

Chats list

All conversations in one list, newest first, with unread counts per chat and a total badge on the tab itself. Start a DM or a group with people from your circles.

Where: Chats tab (bottom bar). New chat from the top of the Chats list.

Message photos, edits, deletes

Attach photos to messages. Edit and delete your own messages, and tap a photo to see it full screen.

Where: Inside any conversation, from the Chats tab.

Find people and keep score

Search

Search people by name, with photos in the results. Tap any result to open that profile. Recent searches are kept (up to 8) so you can rerun one in a tap, and you can clear them.

Where: Magnifier icon at the top of the Drops feed.

Scan a profile code

Scan a friend's PlotDrop QR code to open their profile instantly. Your own code lives on your profile: let a friend scan it with the PlotDrop scanner.

Where: Scan from the Search screen; your code from your Profile.

Weekly leaderboard

The weekly board separates instinct from luck. Climb it with correct calls, and defend your spot when the next drop lands.

Where: Search screen, "Weekly leaderboard".

Notifications

Reveals, likes, follows, comments, join requests, re-drops, messages, and spotlight drops, grouped so you can catch up: fresh reveals, spotlight, and still waiting. Tap a notification to open what it is about.

Where: Bell icon at the top of the screen.

Profiles

Your profile

Photo, display name, handle, title, and points, then your pinned drop at the top, your DROPS & POSTS feed (drops, posts, and re-drops newest-first), and your HALL OF FAME below it.

Where: Profile tab (bottom bar).

Other profiles

Tap any name or photo anywhere (outside circle chats) to open that profile: same layout, same sections. Follow and unfollow from the profile header.

Where: Tap a name or photo on a card, in search, or in a chat.

Settings and account

Settings

Edit profile, read How PlotDrop works and the Terms of Service, set push notification preferences, check This device (including a test push), manage blocked users, and log out. The app version sits at the bottom of the screen.

Where: Profile tab, then Settings.

Every feature, explained

A plain-language guide to every feature in the app, from the Settings screen. No more hunting through tabs to learn what a button does.

Where: Settings, "Every feature, explained".

Delete or deactivate your account

Two choices, both from the same place: Delete in 7 days (a grace period you can cancel any time before it ends) or Delete right now (immediate, cannot be undone). A pending deletion shows a banner on the home feed with a Cancel button right there. Deactivation is separate from deletion.

Where: Settings, then Account.

Getting started

Sign up and sign in

Email and password. PlotDrop is for users aged 13 and over, and signing up asks you to agree to the Terms of Service. Forgot your password or need to verify your email? Both live on the sign-in screen.

Where: The sign-in screen when you open the app signed out.

How PlotDrop works

The in-app walkthrough: read the story, call the twist, wait for the drop. New users with few friends can learn the whole loop by playing the Daily Twist.

Where: Settings, "How PlotDrop works".

Invite friends

Invite people from your contacts with a ready-made message. Your address book is read on your device only: nothing from it leaves your phone.

Where: The Invite screen, linked from the app.

Get the app

Tester installs live here: join the tester group, become a tester, then install. iPhone testers use the TestFlight link; Android testers install from the Play Store tester link.

Where: plotdrop.org/download.
