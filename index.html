How it works

Beat 01

Read the drop

Short stories are posted as drops with the ending held back. You get the setup, the characters, and the tension - everything except how it ends.

Beat 02

Call the twist

Make your prediction and tap I CALLED IT before the deadline locks. Your pick is on the record and your points are on the line.

Beat 03

Wait for the drop

The reveal lands on the countdown. Right calls pay out in points, streaks build, and the leaderboard sorts the prophets from the lucky.

Built for the call

📖Drops & reveals

Stories posted with the ending held back, revealed to everyone at the same moment when the countdown ends. No spoilers, no early peeks.

🎯Predictions & points

Lock your prediction before the deadline. Right calls earn points, and streaks reward players who keep reading the story right.

🏆Leaderboard

A weekly board that separates instinct from luck. Climb it with correct calls - and defend your spot when the next drop lands.

⭕Circles

Private groups for your people. Create a circle, invite your crew, and run drops where the bragging rights actually matter.

💬Direct messages

One-on-one and group chats built in, so the debate, the taunting, and the post-reveal fallout never have to leave the app.

🕊Daily Verse

A new verse drop every morning - a daily return hook with the same call-it mechanic, plus points for engaging with the verse.

🛡Report & block

Safety tools are built in, not bolted on. Report content that breaks the rules and block anyone you don't want to hear from.

Questions

What is PlotDrop?

PlotDrop is a social story-prediction app. Writers post short stories with the ending held back - those are drops. Readers predict how the story ends before a deadline, and when the reveal lands, correct predictions earn points on a weekly leaderboard.

When does PlotDrop launch?

PlotDrop is in closed testing on Google Play right now, and Founding Testers are being recruited - see Become a tester for the 3 steps to get in early. After the 14-day closed test, the public launch opens on Google Play. When the public listing goes live, this page will say so.

Is it Android or iPhone?

Android first, through Google Play. An iPhone version may come later - nothing is in the works for it right now.

Is PlotDrop free?

Yes. Reading drops, making predictions, joining circles, and climbing the leaderboard are free.

Is PlotDrop safe?

PlotDrop is built for ages 13 and up. Every account can report content and block other users, blocked users disappear from your feed and chats, and accounts can be deleted in the app at any time. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for the details.
