Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

PlotDrop ("we", "the app") is a social story-prediction game: users post short stories with a twist, predict whether a story is true or made up, comment, follow each other, and message. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, password (stored securely by our auth provider), display name, username, and profile photo if you add one.
  • Content you create: stories ("drops"), posts, comments, messages (direct and circle/group), photos you upload, predictions, likes, re-drops, and follows.
  • Notification data: a push token for your device so we can send the notifications you choose (reveals, messages, likes, follows).
  • Basic technical logs needed to run and secure the service.

What we do NOT collect

  • No location data. No financial or health information.
  • We never upload your contacts: the invite-friends feature reads your address book on your device only, and nothing from it leaves your phone.
  • No advertising identifiers, and we show no ads.
  • We never sell your personal information.

How we use it

Only to run the app: showing your content to other users as you direct, delivering notifications, keeping accounts secure, and improving the service. Content you post publicly (drops, posts, public comments) is visible to other signed-in users by design.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) and Expo (push notification delivery) strictly to operate the app on our behalf. They do not use your information for their own purposes.

Your choices and deletion

  • You can deactivate or delete your account in the app: Settings -> Account -> Delete account. Deletion is permanent after a 7-day grace period and removes your Auth record, database records, and stored files together.
  • You can turn push notifications off in the app settings or your device.

Children

PlotDrop is for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We will post updates on this page (https://plotdrop.org/privacy) and note the new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@plotdrop.org, or through https://plotdrop.org.
